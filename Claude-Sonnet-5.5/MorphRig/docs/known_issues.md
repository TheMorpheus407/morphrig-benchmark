# Known issues and limits

Measured on the delivered files. Numbers come from the QA scripts in `source/generators/` and from the runs listed in `docs/animation_state_contract.md` and `docs/perf/`. Nothing below hides a missing feature; each item says what is imperfect and by how much.

## Character and rig (Blender)

1. **IK ← FK matching leaves a small roll difference.** After **IK ← FK** on an arm the hand and the elbow match to 0.2 mm and 0.0°, but the upper-arm bone can differ by about 7° of roll about its own axis, because an IK chain derives that roll from the pole. Legs match to 0.1 mm and 0.0°. FK ← IK is exact.
2. **Facial detail is stylised.** The face is a 3 to 5 mm quad mesh with painted brows and lash lines (no hair cards or lash geometry). The nose is modelled after the smoothing pass as a height field (about 2 cm from the face plane at the tip) and the face surface was smoothed by up to 5 mm to remove creases; the eyelid and mouth rings are untouched. 72 of the roughly 3,500 quads on the front of the head stay warped by more than 20 degrees (the worst by 68 degrees at the mouth corner, most beside the eyelid rings, the lip rings and the nose flank) and show as faint creases in a hard grazing light at close range.
3. **Hair is a rigid cap plus a braid.** The scalp cap follows the head bone without simulation; only the braid and the cable move as secondary chains. The cap adds 0.75 cm: the character is 180.05 cm to the top of the skull and 180.8 cm with the hair (`docs/material_and_lod_report.json`).
4. **Skin shading is standard.** No subsurface scattering or wrinkle maps; the normal maps are procedural micro-relief.
5. **LOD1 and LOD2 carry no morph targets.** LOD1 (20,043 triangles) and LOD2 (8,818) keep shape, UVs, materials and skinning, the 44 face morphs exist on LOD0 only. LOD2 shows visible decimation in close-ups of the face and hands (it is meant for distance).
6. **Secondary motion is not baked into the exported clips.** The Unreal controller simulates the braid and the cable at runtime; in Blender the chains can be baked into an action with the tools panel (`Bake secondary motion`). A baked chain would be applied twice if it were exported and simulated again.

## Animation

7. **Floor contact.** Across the 96 clips the lowest mesh point stays within 1.6 cm of the floor in every clip except the three sleep clips: `sleep_start` dips 3.2 cm for about five frames (left forearm blending under the body), `sleep_loop` 2.6 cm and `sleep_end` 2.2 cm (belt pouches resting on the floor while the body lies on its side). The held poses `prone_*` and `dead_*` end within 1.2 cm.
8. **Foot skating.** During stance the ball bone follows the constant-speed ground prediction within 2.9 cm (`walk_f`), 1.9 cm (`run_*`) and 2.3 cm (`sprint_f`); the measure includes the heel-to-toe roll of the foot, so the planted contact point itself slides less.
9. **Speed of the fastest motions.** Between two frames (at 30 fps) the head, pelvis, hands, feet, forearms, calves, chest and blade never move more than 43 cm in any clip outside the attacks, dashes, the blink arrival and the running pivot (`channel_interrupt` left hand 43 cm, `knockup_start` 43, `cast_directional` 43, `jump_start` 42, `land_heavy` 40). Those are fast by design: the blade tip moves 78 cm per frame in `melee_2`, 63 in `melee_3` and 61 in `melee_1`, the calf 65 cm in `dash_f`, the feet 49 to 51 cm in the other dashes, the hands 58 cm in `blink_in`, and the swing-leg knee 45 cm in `pivot_r180`. The right hand of `cast_self` stops within one frame after a 33 cm step, which is the intended burst.
10. **Hit reactions and disables are single takes.** Front/back/left/right hit reactions, stun, knockback and knockup are authored once; the controller mixes them additively or blends into them.
11. **The speech performance is one authored take.** One synthesised voice (public-domain LJ Speech training data, an audiobook-style read), five sentences, 16.6 s. The lip shapes follow the exact phoneme durations of the synthesis. The independent recognition check (Whisper `small.en`) finds all 39 words; its word boundaries differ from the alignment by a median of 0.11 s at word starts and 0.05 s at word ends. Whisper's own timing is the coarser of the two.
12. **Regenerating the speech gives a different take.** The voice model adds random noise. The shipped WAV, transcript and alignment belong together.

## Materials and textures

13. **Some UV triangles overlap in hidden places.** The skin atlas has no overlapping triangles (the faces inside the eye pockets and the mouth cavity are unwrapped as islands of their own). 7 triangles of the suit (the right shoulder blade under the pauldron and the neck base under the collar) and 2 triangles of the cybernetic forearm (the wrist under the hand) still overlap in UV space; all of them lie under other parts and the textures there are smooth.
14. **Textures are procedural.** All maps come from `paint.py`; no photographs. The team icons (circle for team A, diamond for team B) sit on the chest plate, the back plate and the top of the right shoulder guard.
15. **`export/textures` is a link.** It is a relative symbolic link to `source/textures/`, so the folder holds one copy of each texture and a file copy of `export/` on a system without symbolic links loses it (copy `source/textures/` instead).

## Unreal showcase and controller

16. **The packaged client is a Development build.** `tools/build_linux.sh` cooks and packages `-clientconfig=Development` (pak and IoStore), so the log and the console stay available. A Shipping package was not built and not measured.
17. **The animation controller is code, not an Animation Blueprint.** `FOperativeAnimProxy::Evaluate` returns true and builds the pose from the state recipe (`docs/animation_state_contract.md` sections 1 and 2), so the project contains no Animation Blueprint asset. Skeleton, clips, notifies and sockets are ordinary assets; a team that wants a graph has to port `Evaluate`.
18. **Foot IK is a per foot two-bone solve on a line trace,** not a full-body solver. Measured in the packaged client (planted feet, mean height error with IK on and off): ramp of 20 degrees 1.92 cm and 12.21 cm with a peak penetration of 0.00 cm and 15.72 cm; five stairs of 20 cm 0.79 cm and 1.88 cm, penetration 0.02 cm and 2.30 cm; curbs of 10, 15 and 20 cm 0.47 cm and 1.97 cm, penetration 1.81 cm and 6.81 cm. A planted foot floats at most 3.3 cm above the surface, and the pelvis moves by up to 26.6 cm on the stairs (it follows the lowest foot). Slopes above 32 degrees and steps above 20 cm are not walkable by design.
19. **Secondary motion has no collision.** The braid and the cable are position-based spring chains with pinned roots and fixed bone lengths; they do not collide with the body, so nothing keeps the braid out of the back plate in an extreme pose. The showcase does not measure this.
20. **Dash and knockback move the capsule with a custom movement mode.** The capsule follows the authored root motion of the dash clips (200 cm, measured 200.0 and 199.5 cm along the dash axis for the four dashes in the sequence) or the knockback profile (250 cm against 260 cm). Obstacles fall back on the standard step and slide rules of `MoveAlongFloor`; a dash into a wall is not part of the sequence.
21. **The team outline is an inverted hull.** It is an overlay material (2.2 px, solid for team A and dashed for team B) and costs a second draw of the character; the face view draws without it. The suit icons carry the team identity without the outline.
22. **Only Linux was built.** The C++ has one platform specific block (`#if PLATFORM_UNIX`, the host load line of the frame time logs), but a Windows package was not produced or tested, and the packaged client ran on one machine only (NixOS, NVIDIA RTX 5090, Vulkan).
23. **Frame times depend on the host.** The logs in `docs/perf/` state the hardware, settings and the host load of the run. Other processes on the shared machine change the game and render thread columns by a large factor while the GPU column moves by about 10 percent (`docs/perf/README.md`).
