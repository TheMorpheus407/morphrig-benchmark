## Deformation and contact corrections

Blender and Unreal now both use **linear blend skinning**. Earlier dual-quaternion previews concealed a mechanical-elbow collapse and shoulder cap intersections in native rendering. The final mechanical sleeve/forearm follow their respective rigid bones, and the shoulder cuffs have intentional open ends with clearance. The generator, master source and production source contain these corrections. The actual packaged [death_front at 1.20 s](final_skin_death/inspection.png) retains the elbow cross-section; [respawn at 0.33 s](final_skin_respawn/inspection.png) has clean cuff borders. The same repaired silhouettes are visible in final motion frames 750 and 820.

The organic forearm/palm seam was corrected by matching their endpoint weights. The final cell casing has a tapered wrist-facing end, correcting the earlier visible wrist penetration without altering motion transforms. Independent scans cover **all 70 reload frames and all 55 deploy frames on every LOD**: zero hand/wrist vertices inside the closed prop bodies and zero intersecting hand/prop surface triangle pairs. LOD0 held contact is 0.264–2.224 mm for reload and at most 0.122 mm for deploy; the corresponding LOD2 maxima are 3.591 mm and 0.724 mm. [LOD0 scan](acceptance/contact_surfaces.json), [LOD1](acceptance/LOD1/contact_surfaces.json), [LOD2](acceptance/LOD2/contact_surfaces.json), [final reload side view](reload_cell_after/side.png), [deploy opposing-thumb view](acceptance/deploy_20_contact.png).

The dense floor regression covers every frame of 14 floor/contact clips and every mesh vertex, including fingers, blade and cable. Minimum Z is no lower than −0.000000164 m, a numerical tolerance. Earlier blade, knee, hand and beacon-floor penetrations are corrected. [Final floor/contact source summary](source_final_summary.md), [dense scan](../floor_clip_scan.json). These are sampled 30 FPS contact checks rather than continuous swept-collision proofs.

## Native integration and rendered runtime review

The final packaged Vulkan client launches without the editor and loads all 96 animation assets. Native LODs contain **42,864 /19,225 /11,236 triangles**, with 39,898 /19,748 /12,383 render vertices and eight sections each. Native counts reflect degenerate removal and UV/normal splits. Reference height is 180 cm with 64 bones. [Packaged inventory](../unreal_native_mesh_report.json), [actual final smoke image](final_verified_package/inspection.png).

The explicit coordinate conversion is `(−Y,−X,Z)×100`. The final compressed-native comparison samples 18 poses across six clips; maximum bone-position error is **0.005600755 cm**. The 2 m dash becomes approximately 200 cm along native+X without a runtime mesh-yaw repair. [Source/native comparison](../unreal_source_pose_comparison.json).

Actual UI tests exercise search/playback, camera/face modes, LOD switching, presets, individual blink/viseme/eye controls, modifiers, stasis/death priority and reset. The final foreground skeleton overlay was verified through real keyboard input in the [packaged channel pose](input_focus_probe/01_activate_keyboard.png). State/event evidence records loaded 96/96 assets, deterministic sequence completion and no duplicate marker execution per generation/loop. [Validation index](README.md), [observed sequence audit](runtime_delivery_demo/trace_audit.json), [real input test](scroll_stasis_escape/state_event_trace.log). Event traces establish execution; rendered contact is assessed separately.

## Presentation, performance and scope

The 8-second turntable and 14-second face video were regenerated from the final source hash at 1920×1080,30 FPS. Their actual decoded frames were independently re-inspected, including the final shoulders, elbow and mouth/eye articulation. [Stream/hash checks](source_media_final.json), [media review](source_media_review.md). Final motion and terrain captures use the packaged Unreal renderer; their fixed 30 FPS capture clock is excluded from performance evidence.

Measured final packaged wall-clock performance at 1920×1080, after discarding the first 10 seconds, is 627.7 FPS for one Operative and 578.7 FPS for ten. The p95 frame times are 3.447 ms and 2.749 ms on the supplied Ryzen 9950X/RTX 5090 host. The single close view uses LOD0; the wider ten-actor view uses automatic LOD1 for the hero. [Measured report and raw-data references](../performance_report.md). These results apply to the recorded showcase settings and hardware.

Earlier diagnostic folders retain the defects that prompted corrections; they are historical evidence, not unresolved source failures. This review covers complete action/asset inventories and the explicitly listed dense probes, with sampled rendered inspection. It does not exhaust all combinations of controls or arbitrary new rig edits. The local eSpeak voice is intentionally synthetic. Lighting differs between the Blender source videos and Unreal runtime. See [known issues](../known_issues.md) and the [validation index](README.md) for the final delivery scope and artifact map.
